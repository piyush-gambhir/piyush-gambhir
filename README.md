I am a software engineer based in Delhi, India. At [Spyne](https://www.spyne.ai), I work on conversational voice and chat agents for automotive retail.

My interests sit at the intersection of **agentic systems and production engineering**: making AI useful beyond a demo, giving it reliable tools, and keeping the systems around it observable, testable, and understandable. I also enjoy taking products from a rough idea to a complete interface, API, deployment, and operating model.

## What I care about

- **Useful AI, not AI theatre** — agents grounded in real tools, workflows, and product constraints.
- **Operability by design** — observability, safe defaults, explicit boundaries, and good failure modes.
- **Small tools with sharp interfaces** — composable CLIs, APIs, workers, and local-first utilities.
- **End-to-end ownership** — product thinking, frontend craft, backend architecture, and deployment all matter.
- **Learning in public** — open-source projects, experiments, technical notes, and reusable playbooks.

## The threads running through my work

### Agents, voice, and real-time systems

I am interested in agents that can listen, see, reason, and act while remaining inspectable. That shows up in conversational AI at work and in experiments such as [the-noir-game](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/the-noir-game), [SÉANCE](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/seance), the [telephony backend](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/telephony), and the [voice simulator](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/telephony-voice-simulator).

### Infrastructure and developer experience

I build tools that reduce the friction of operating software: [infra-lab](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/infra-lab) for ready-to-run infrastructure, [reckon](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/reckon) for agent-assisted production investigation, and a suite of focused Go CLIs for [Grafana](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/grafana-cli), [Jenkins](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/jenkins-cli), [Elasticsearch](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/es-cli), [Jira](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/jira-cli), [Nginx Proxy Manager](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/nginxpm-cli), and [CubeAPM](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/cubeapm-cli).

### Products, interfaces, and automation

I like turning infrastructure into something people can actually use. Recent work spans the [Nexora marketplace prototype](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/nexora-marketplace), [AI quiz generation](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/quiz-it-now), the local-first [Perch tab manager](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/perch), [browser automation](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/google-reviews-scraping), and reusable starters for [Next.js](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/nextjs-template), [FastAPI](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/fastapi-boilerplate), and [browser extensions](https://github.com/piyush-gambhir/wxt-web-extension-react-ts-boilerplate).
